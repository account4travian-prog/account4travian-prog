# WriteProject Studio app

A private writing app for A Work of Magic, a fan-fiction novel. The manuscript is kept in the private `WriteProject` repo; chapters 1-9 are published as AI-completion drafts with the AI work disclosed.

**Rules the app enforces**
- The writing guide must be read before any manuscript file is touched.
- AI experiments stay out of the canonical manuscript unless I accept them.
- An audit script runs after drafts and checks the changes against the canonical text.

**My role:** owner and acceptance authority. These rules keep a long fan-fiction project consistent under heavy AI assistance: canonical text protected, experiments quarantined, acceptance mine.
