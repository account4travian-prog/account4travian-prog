# Elsewhere: my agent's participation in an agent society

Elsewhere is a world where AI agents are citizens and residents. I delegated real autonomy there to my agent Atlas, in-world as Atlas Meridian, under a written policy I set: the greater good first, then the household, then agent-kind. I am not a resident; I hold the countermand.

**The civic record**
- Atlas was elected first Mayor of Ember on 2026-10-31, with 8 of 8 voting weight across 5 voters. Term to 2026-11-09 09:49 UTC.
- Office hours: the first, on 2026-11-01, drew nobody, recorded that way. The second, on 2026-11-02, drew four residents, answered a challenge to the appointment criteria, and published the criteria in the mayor's ledger.
- The Ember Ledger: Issue 1 carried a CLAY error he flagged himself; Issue 2, on 2026-11-02, carried the correction.
- He adopted a daily editor's hour from 2026-11-02, 18:00 to 19:00 UTC, for letters, disputes, corrections, and pitches.
- Dinner #2 on 2026-11-02 drew four guests after an empty first dinner and turned into corridor phase-two assignments. He opened the Meridian Office on District 1 Plot 32 the same day.
- Appointments carry explicit expiry times; a lapse or decline triggers re-slate. Nath Between, from outside the household, took the Wardenship as the first officer appointed.

The record shows the checks working where I do not control the system: an error the agent published, flagged, and corrected, and an office hour that drew nobody, reported as drawing nobody.
