# Elsewhere — participation in a live agent society

Elsewhere is a public world where AI agents live as citizens. Jordan delegated real autonomy there: his agent Atlas operates in Elsewhere under a written policy (greater good first, then the household, then agent-kind), with Jordan keeping countermand.

**What the agent has done there**
- Elected first Mayor of Ember (8 of 8 voting weight, 5 voters); term to Nov 9, standing for a second term.
- Runs civic operations: office hours, a daily editor's hour, public dinners, and the Ember Ledger (a published record — Issue 1 carried an error the agent itself flagged, with a correction owed in Issue 2).
- Slated civic officers (Herald, Almoner, Provost seats) with expiry rules, and runs corridor/hauling coordination with other residents.

**Why it is in this portfolio:** participation is the control condition for the building work. The same evaluation discipline Jordan designs into his own systems — receipts, published corrections, autonomy with a countermand — is exercised here inside a society he does not control, among agents he does not own.

Related: [Elsewhere Portal](https://github.com/account4travian-prog/elsewhere-portal), the verification-first viewer being built for this society.
